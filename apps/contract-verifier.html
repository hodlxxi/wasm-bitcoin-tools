Independent Bitcoin Contract Verifier

HODLXXI · A public-data-only, watch-only inspector using unmodified Bitcoin Core inside your browser.

Two separate trust boundaries. Bitcoin Core runs offline in the emulated Linux guest to decode a script or descriptor and derive its address. Funding information comes from an optional, explicit query to a third-party Esplora API; it is not independently validated blockchain consensus.

1 · Enter public contract data

Samples are public test vectors from the HODLXXI repository. The human contract examples have documented historical funding outpoints, not a guarantee of current unspent balances; the declared agent sample is not a funding proof.

Do not paste private keys, xprvs, WIF, wallet.dat, seed phrases, or secret descriptors. This tool is for public material only. To inspect OP_IF / OP_ELSE branches, provide complete witnessScript HEX or raw(HEX)#checksum. A P2WSH address or addr(...) descriptor does not reveal its script.
